Checked 2026-10-20 against the [official call](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-demonstrations). These are track requirements, not assumptions from other ICSE tracks:

- Use `\documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran}`; exclude `compsoc`/`compsocconf`. Title: 24 pt; main text: 10 pt. Preserve prescribed layout.
- Submit PDF, at most four pages total, including references, figures, tables, appendices.
- Single-anonymous review: include author identities.

- Link the public tool and usage instructions; source repository is optional. Provide an easy-to-use distribution; reviewers should not have to build source.

- Review considers relevance, soundness, novelty, video presentation, usefulness, and related literature.

## Editorial choices for this project

These are working choices, not conference rules: prefer one workflow/architecture figure, one focused screenshot if it adds evidence, and one compact evidence table. Allocate space to the demonstration and implementation, with concise motivation and related work. Keep limitations adjacent to claims. Adjust this balance to the actual argument rather than filling a fixed section template.
